# Project — Agenda XPTO

## Vision
Professionalizing scheduling for small beauty and aesthetic establishments. Agenda XPTO eliminates the friction of manual booking via WhatsApp or physical notebooks, providing a seamless digital experience for both owners and clients.

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Micro and small beauty/aesthetic establishments.
- **Examples:** Barbershops, Hair Salons, Nail/Manicure Studios, Tattoo & Piercing Studios, Aesthetic Clinics.
- **Key User (Owner):** Typically works alone or with a small team (1-3 staff), values simplicity, and wants to reduce no-shows.

## Business Model
**SaaS (Subscription as a Service)** with tiered monthly plans:
- **Starter (R$ 59/mo):** 1 establishment, up to 2 professionals, up to 100 appointments/month.
- **Pro (R$ 129/mo):** Up to 3 establishments, up to 10 professionals/establishment, unlimited appointments.
- **Business (R$ 249/mo):** Up to 10 establishments, unlimited professionals, unlimited appointments.

### Trial Policy
- 15-day **Pro Trial** for new users.
- No credit card required upfront.
- Automatic downgrade to **Starter** if trial expires without conversion.

## Core Value Proposition
- **For Owners:** Automated reminders, professional presence, zero-effort scheduling.
- **For Clients:** Book in seconds, 24/7, with instant confirmation and easy cancellation.
