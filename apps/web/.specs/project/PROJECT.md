# Agenda XPTO - Frontend

## Vision & Goals
Agenda XPTO is a SaaS platform for scheduling management targeting small beauty and aesthetic establishments (barbershops, hair salons, nail studios, tattoo studios). 

The goal is to provide a professional, automated booking experience for clients and an efficient administrative panel for owners, reducing no-shows and manual scheduling work.

### Core Goals
- **Self-Service Booking:** 80%+ of appointments made autonomously by clients via the public page.
- **Rapid Setup:** Owners can configure and activate their business in under 30 minutes.
- **Multi-tenancy:** Manage multiple establishments with a single login.
- **SSR Performance:** Leverage TanStack Start for fast initial loads and SEO-friendly booking pages.

## Success Metrics
- Reduction of ≥ 30% in no-shows through automated reminders.
- ≥ 40% of bookings made outside of business hours.
- High usability scores on mobile devices for both owners and clients.

## Target Audience
- **Owners:** Micro and small beauty/aesthetic business owners (1-3 employees).
- **Clients:** Customers seeking beauty services, prioritizing speed and 24/7 availability.

## Scope (MVP)
- Public booking page (Client-facing).
- Admin panel (Dashboard, Services, Professionals, Availability, Appointments).
- Multi-establishment support.
- Email notifications/reminders.

## Out of Scope (Post-MVP)
- WhatsApp integration & AI assistant.
- IA credits & FAQ Knowledge Base.
- Online payments.
- Playwright E2E (Unit/Integration focus for now).
